Overview

A new era of Pro.

The iPhone 15 Pro is powered by the A17 Pro processor — the world's first 3-nanometer chip in a smartphone. With support for USB 3 via USB-C, a 48MP main camera, and a 12MP Ultra Wide, it redefines what a Pro phone can do. The Dynamic Island continues Apple's signature interface innovation, while the border narrows to just 1.55mm.

Design

Aerospace-grade titanium.

iPhone 15 Pro is the first iPhone to feature an aerospace-grade titanium design, using the same alloy that spacecraft use for missions to Mars.

Titanium has one of the best strength-to-weight ratios of any metal, making these our lightest Pro models ever. You'll notice the difference the moment you pick one up.

1.55mm border — thinnest ever on iPhone Pro
